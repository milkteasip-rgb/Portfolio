I'm a visual designer at Western Digital, trained at ArtCenter College of Design in the visual fundamentals I still lean on for every project.

Craft is where my focus is right now. That includes using AI, mostly generating and prompting imagery, and staying curious about how AI tools could make a designer's workflow easier. I want my work to hold up past the first glance, not just look good in a mockup. I've also started paying more attention to research and the UX/UI process, the thinking behind a design before it gets made, and I look for chances to learn more about it along the way. I ask real questions, push ideas further, and bring people along when I see a better direction.
