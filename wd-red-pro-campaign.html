WD Red Pro Campaign

Developed the creative direction and campaign assets for WD Red Pro, translating key product benefits into a cohesive visual system.

OwnershipCreative direction, key art, and campaign assets
Year2026
AudienceB2B customers
Challenge

The key art had to be understood at a glance, restrained enough for a business audience, and clear about what the product does and what it’s used for.

Solution

I made the product the hero, so the concept reads instantly with no decoration competing for attention. I then placed it in a server room, where it’s actually used, so the viewer understands its purpose without a caption. Keeping the visual language simple made it feel credible to business buyers instead of flashy.
